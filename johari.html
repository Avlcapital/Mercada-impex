Johari Gin

The Pride of Tanzania – A Premium African Spirit Crafted with Heritage.

Our Origins

Johari Gin was born in the heart of Tanzania, crafted with a vision to create a spirit that not only delights the palate but also tells the story of African pride. Its name, “Johari,” meaning “jewel” in Swahili, reflects the precious nature of this gin – a jewel of the African beverage industry. From the beginning, our mission has been clear: to introduce the world to a premium African spirit that competes with the best, while remaining authentic to our roots and traditions.

A Unique Blend

What makes Johari Gin truly special is its distinct blend of locally sourced botanicals. Using Tanzania’s rich biodiversity, we carefully select juniper, cardamom, citrus peels, and native herbs to create a flavor that is crisp, balanced, and refreshing. Distilled in small batches with copper stills and pure mountain spring water, Johari Gin delivers a smooth texture and refined taste that appeals globally.

Celebrating Africa

Johari Gin is not just a drink; it is a celebration of Africa’s growing voice in the global craft beverage scene. Each sip represents centuries of tradition, storytelling, and the resilience of African people. Through Johari Gin, we aim to redefine how the world sees African spirits — bold, original, and proudly ours.

Global Recognition

In just a few years, Johari Gin has begun to gain recognition beyond Tanzania. From Africa’s bustling capitals to Europe’s gin festivals, our spirit is being praised for originality and quality. Each export bottle carries the Tanzanian flag higher, positioning Johari as Africa’s jewel in the world of premium spirits.
