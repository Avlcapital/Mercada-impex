Johari Gin

The Pride of Tanzania - A Premium African Spirit Crafted with Heritage.

Our Origins

Johari Gin was born in the heart of Tanzania, crafted with a vision to create a spirit that not only delights the palate but also tells the story of African pride. Its name, “Johari,” meaning “jewel” in Swahili, reflects the precious nature of this gin - a jewel of the African beverage industry. From the beginning, our mission has been clear: to introduce the world to a premium African spirit that competes with the best, while remaining authentic to our roots and traditions. Every bottle carries with it a legacy of Tanzanian culture and the entrepreneurial spirit of Africa rising on the global stage.

A Unique Blend

What makes Johari Gin truly special is its distinct blend of locally sourced botanicals. Using Tanzania’s rich biodiversity, we carefully select juniper, cardamom, citrus peels, and native herbs to create a flavor that is crisp, balanced, and refreshing. The gin is distilled in small batches using traditional copper stills, ensuring that every bottle receives the attention it deserves. Combined with pure mountain spring water, Johari Gin delivers a smooth texture and refined taste that appeals to both casual drinkers and connoisseurs alike. Whether served neat, on ice, or in cocktails, it brings out the authentic essence of Africa.

Celebrating Africa

Johari Gin is not just a drink; it is a celebration of Africa’s growing voice in the global craft beverage scene. Each sip represents centuries of tradition, storytelling, and the resilience of African people. It captures the warmth of Tanzanian hospitality, the vibrance of African culture, and the passion of artisans who dedicate themselves to excellence. Through Johari Gin, we aim to redefine how the world sees African spirits — not as imitations of Western brands, but as bold, original creations that carry our own identity and cultural pride. Drinking Johari Gin is more than tasting a beverage — it is experiencing Africa.

Global Recognition

In just a few years, Johari Gin has begun to gain recognition beyond Tanzania. From Africa’s bustling capitals to Europe’s gin festivals, our spirit is being praised for its originality and quality. Distributors and bartenders across the world are taking note, adding Johari Gin to their menus as a premium choice for customers seeking something different, something truly authentic. Our goal is not just to be another gin on the shelf, but to become a symbol of Africa’s ability to produce world-class beverages. With every bottle exported, we carry the Tanzanian flag higher, showing that Africa’s “jewel” deserves its place in the global spotlight.
