Johari Gin

The Pride of Tanzania — A Premium African Spirit Crafted with Heritage.

Our Origins

Johari Gin is a Tanzanian masterpiece — a spirit born out of passion and African pride. Distilled with carefully selected botanicals from East Africa, it delivers a refreshing taste that speaks of culture, heritage, and authenticity.

A Unique Blend

Crafted using local spices and pure spring water, Johari Gin captures the richness of Tanzania’s natural bounty. Its balanced flavor makes it ideal for cocktails, celebrations, and premium experiences worldwide.

Celebrating Africa

Johari is more than just a drink — it is a celebration of Africa’s rising craft beverage industry. Every sip tells the story of tradition meeting innovation, proudly representing Tanzania on the global stage.

Global Recognition

With growing international recognition, Johari Gin is gaining popularity among spirit enthusiasts across Africa, Europe, and beyond. Its distinct African identity positions it as a trailblazer in premium spirits.
